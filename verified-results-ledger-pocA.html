PoC A · Focus Area A · Pay-for-results WASH

One water point. Three witnesses. One payment.

A results-based contract only works when the payer trusts the number. This demonstrator runs the Akvo Verification Protocol (AVP) over one quarter of an area-service-provider contract: every water point's claim is checked against independent evidence, given a confidence grade, and turned into a payment the ministry's auditor can trace line by line. Switch the evidence tier to see what verification costs — and what each tier fails to catch.

AssumptionsNakisi District cluster is fictional; all 124 water points, evidence streams and cash figures are synthetic and illustrative. Payment rules mirror the Uganda area-service-provider design (fixed viability-gap subsidy + performance incentive on reliability, revenue collection and water quality) but are not that contract's terms. Tier costs are order-of-magnitude placeholders pending the verification-cost benchmark. Rule thresholds are proposals for the authoring group (Aquaya, Uptime/Instiglio, Oxford), not agreed values.
