Benchmark Concept · July 2026

What does verification actually cost — per site, per year?

The OECD (2026, Financing Water Security) names verification cost as the main barrier to scaling results-based finance — and notes that almost no cost data is published. This benchmark compares the three verification approaches available to funders and RBF facilities. Adjust the programme parameters; the model recalculates annual verification cost, cost per site, and what each approach leaves behind.

Evidence continuity
Consultant: point-in-time. CLEM: continuous with quarterly validation. EO: continuous, parameter-limited. RBF payers disburse quarterly — snapshot evidence forces payment on stale data.
Capacity legacy
Only CLEM leaves trained community monitors and a functioning local data chain after the contract — the OECD's "required capacity" gap. Consultant and EO-only approaches leave nothing behind.
Best fit
Consultant: one-off endline evaluations. CLEM: NbS/carbon claims, RBF portfolios with community presence. EO-only: fragile contexts, water-body scale results, measurement recovery.
