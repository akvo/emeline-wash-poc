02How it works
the SPEAK pipeline, adapted to the water domain
voice → ledger → finance
Step 1 — Speak

Caretakers & treasurers speak

After fee collection, a breakdown, or a repair, they send a voice note in their own language via WhatsApp or Viber. No forms, no literacy barrier, no new hardware.

Step 2 — Structure

AI structures the data

An NLP engine extracts amounts, dates, and events into standardised digital ledgers. Area mechanics and district officers verify flagged entries — human-in-the-loop.

Step 3 — Serve

Insights are delivered

Committees get simple monthly voice/visual summaries; districts get live functionality & revenue dashboards; lenders get verified service reliability records.

PROOF POINT In Uganda, structured record keeping turned smallholder farmers into bankable borrowers — 99% loan approval, 3% default vs. ~20% regional baseline. SPEAK-Water applies the same de-risking logic to the rural water maintenance gap (~25% of handpumps non-functional at any time).

Design principle

Voice-first, zero friction

No app to install, no forms, no literacy requirement. Structured audio prompts guide complete entries — the same approach piloted with farmer cohorts in SPEAK.

Data quality

Confirm-back loop

The AI reads back what it understood before saving. Uncertain entries are flagged for the area mechanic or district officer to verify — human-in-the-loop, like extension agents in the agri pilot.

Immediate value

The farmer gets insight; so does the treasurer

Monthly balance, savings-toward-repairs progress, and comparison to similar committees — delivered as voice notes and simple visuals, so record keeping pays back immediately.

Side effect

Breakdowns become tickets

Any issue mentioned in a voice note automatically notifies the mechanic network and appears on the district dashboard — cutting downtime without a separate reporting channel.

The twist vs. SPEAK-Water

Every job is a disposal receipt

Geostamped voice note + treatment-plant intake confirmation turn each load into a verified safe-disposal event — the SDG 6.3 data unit that results-based funders can pay against (and an emerging methane/carbon angle).

One voice note, three buyers

Entrepreneur · City · Funder

The operator builds a credit record toward equipment loans and a municipal licence; the city gets emptying coverage and illegal-dumping hotspots for CWIS planning; funders get verifiable service units.

Positioning discipline

Generation ≠ verification

The operator writes their own books. Plant receipts, geostamps and spot audits are the independent validation layer — ledger cohorts create demand for verification services rather than replacing them.
